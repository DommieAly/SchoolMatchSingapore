Terms and privacy

What this site is, what it stores, where your data goes and how to remove it.

What this site is

This is an NTU SC2006 student prototype, not an official service of NTU, MOE or any government agency.

PSLE ranges are historical and are not a promise of a place. About the data says where they come from.

Use made-up details only

Use made-up details only. Do not enter a real child's score, school or home address.

This site is for parents and guardians, and for students aged 13 or over. It is not meant for children under 13.

How to remove your data

You can clear your score, school and address on your profile page: empty the field and press Save profile. Clearing the address also clears its coordinates.

You can remove schools on your shortlist and choice plan pages. A choice plan keeps the score and posting group it was made for, even after you clear them on your profile.

To delete a whole account, or to remove that copy from a choice plan, write to contact address.

All accounts and all data are deleted when the course project ends.

What is stored

The database holds these fields. Only the username, email address and password are needed to register; everything else is there only if you enter it.

Account
A random account id, username, a lower-case copy of the username, email address, hashed password (a one-way code, never the password itself), when the account was created, and its status (active or inactive)
Profile
Display name, PSLE score, posting group, primary school, home address and its coordinates (latitude and longitude), CCA and programme choices, usual travel mode, and commute limit
Shortlist
The schools you saved, and when the list last changed
Choice plan
Your ranked schools, the PSLE score and posting group the plan was made for, and when it last changed
Login
A random login id, when the login started and expires, and whether you logged out. Ended logins are deleted about once an hour.

The server also keeps your starting point (a typed address or device position), address matches and latest recommendations in memory, not in the database. They go when you log out or after 30 minutes without activity. Separately, the server keeps each typed address with OneMap's answer for up to 24 hours, and travel-time answers with the rounded starting point for up to 30 minutes, so the same search does not cost a second call. These copies carry no name or account, and they go when the server restarts. The site sets two cookies, one to keep you logged in and one for these page details. It sets no advertising or analytics cookies. To slow down anyone who sends too many requests, the server also counts some requests by IP address. These counts stay in memory for about one hour. If the site is down for a moment, the HTTPS proxy's error log can keep your IP address, your browser's name, and the address of the page you asked for and of the page you came from, with any score or search words in those addresses, until the server is deleted.

Where your data goes

  • Data is stored on a server in Singapore (Amazon Web Services).
  • Typed addresses and postal codes go to OneMap (Singapore Land Authority), to find their location.
  • Locations go to Google, to work out travel times and directions. Google gets map coordinates, with the starting point rounded to about 100 m, and not your name or account.
  • Pages with a map load it from Google in your browser, so Google can see your IP address and the area you view.
  • Every page loads its styles and scripts from jsDelivr, a public file service, which can see your IP address.

These services handle what they receive under their own terms. We cannot delete it for you.
