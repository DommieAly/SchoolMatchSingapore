  • School coordinates and address search: OneMap, Singapore Land Authority.
  • PSLE score ranges and affiliated primary schools: MOE SchoolFinder (moe.gov.sg, Ministry of Education), read on 3 Oct 2026 (2025 S1 posting).
  • School codes in page addresses (e.g. catholic-high-school) are SchoolMatch's own ids, made from the school names. They are not MOE codes.
