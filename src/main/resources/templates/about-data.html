Licences and credits

  • Contains information from General information of schools accessed on 2 Oct 2026 from data.gov.sg which is made available under the terms of the Singapore Open Data Licence version 1.0 (https://data.gov.sg/open-data-licence).
  • Contains information from OneMap search accessed on 3 Oct 2026 from OneMap (onemap.gov.sg, Singapore Land Authority) which is made available under the terms of the Singapore Open Data Licence version 1.0 (https://www.onemap.gov.sg/legal/opendatalicence.html).
  • School coordinates and address search: OneMap, Singapore Land Authority.
  • PSLE score ranges and affiliated primary schools: MOE SchoolFinder (moe.gov.sg, Ministry of Education), read on 3 Oct 2026 (2025 S1 posting).
  • School codes in page addresses (e.g. catholic-high-school) are SchoolMatch's own ids, made from the school names. They are not MOE codes.
  • Maps, routes and nearby places: Google Maps Platform (not stored in this snapshot).
