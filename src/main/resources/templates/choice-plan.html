How the chance is worked out

U = the upper score of the school's latest range for your posting group (the affiliated range if your primary school is affiliated). A lower AL score is better.

  • SAFE your score ≤ U − 2
  • MATCH U − 2 < your score ≤ U
  • REACH your score > U
