How the outputs are written:

- **Table 1** (black box): `6 results | #1 fit-u13 total 0.786, 15 min: FIT 1.0 MATCH; COMMUTE 0.5 | ...` gives the number of results, then for each result its rank, school code, total score, travel time (`commute n/a` when it is not known) and the score of each factor. `FIT` is PSLE fit with its label, `PROG` is programme, and `FIT not used` is the DC-74 reason. `black-box-recommendation.md` Table 5 has the full key.

The calls that follow show the path, for example `getSchools 1x` (called once). `basis-path-recommend.md` section 6.3 has the full key.
- **Table 3** (basis paths of `login`): the result, then `lookups` (account lookups), `checks` (password and status checks) and `saved` (sessions saved). `basis-path-login.md` section 7 has the full key.
